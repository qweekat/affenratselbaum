Meal Plan Checker

Checks food against your allergy list, the Break the Mold Tier 1 rules, and the limits in your eating plan. The rules are fixed, so the same meal always gets the same answer.

What it found

Your text, marked

This checks words, not amounts. It can't tell how much sugar is in a dish or spot an ingredient a recipe leaves out. To check amounts, fiber, and glycemic load, use Build and score a day.

To make it fit

This looks at which foods are in the meal, not the amounts. For exact fiber and glycemic load, add the meal in Build and score a day.

Glycemic load is GI × available carbs (carbs minus fiber) ÷ 100. Per meal, 10 or less is low, 11 to 19 is medium, and 20 or more is high. Your provider hasn't set a carb target yet, so these are general bands, not your limit.

GI values come from published international GI tables (the University of Sydney GI database and Atkinson et al. 2008). Foods marked est. have no tested value, so the number is a stand-in. Non-starchy vegetables, proteins, and fiber supplements count as near zero. Look up any food at glycemicindex.com.

Carb and fiber numbers are approximate, not brand specific. The rules come from your Food Issues list, the Break the Mold handout, and your eating plan. This is a food tool, not medical advice.
